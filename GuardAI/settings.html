Connect to your company

If your workplace runs GuardAI, enter the invite code your admin gave you. There is no account and no password: the code is the only step, and you can disconnect whenever you like.

Once connected, your admin can see how many items were masked and what kind they were, on which AI platform and when. They cannot see the values themselves, the messages around them, or anything you typed. Those never leave this device, connected or not.

Connected to your company Your masking activity is visible to your admin: counts and categories only, never the values or your messages.

Every category GuardAI can detect

All categories are on by default. Turning one off means GuardAI stops looking for it entirely — it won't be masked, and it won't be flagged in the warning card either. This changes what's detected; it doesn't change how each category is recognised.

Changes apply immediately — no need to reload the page you're chatting on.
