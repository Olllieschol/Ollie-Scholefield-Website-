Activate GuardAI

GuardAI needs a key before it will mask anything. Enter your personal licence key, or the invite code your workplace gave you — one field takes either, and there is no account and no password to set up.

A personal licence reports nothing. No counts, no categories, no usage — GuardAI does not contact us again except to check the key is still valid.

A workplace invite code does report counts. Your admin can see how many items were masked and what kind they were, on which AI platform and when. They cannot see the values themselves, the messages around them, or anything you typed. Those never leave this device either way.

Your seat id

This random id is the only thing that identifies your seat to us. It is not linked to your name, your email or your device, so we cannot look you up any other way. Send it to hello@guard4ai.com to get a copy of what is stored against it, or to have it deleted. See the privacy policy.

Every category GuardAI can detect

All categories are on by default. Turning one off means GuardAI stops looking for it entirely — it won't be masked, and it won't be flagged in the warning card either. This changes what's detected; it doesn't change how each category is recognised.

Changes apply immediately — no need to reload the page you're chatting on.
